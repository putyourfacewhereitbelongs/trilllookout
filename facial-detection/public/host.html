How to run Trill Lookout AI Cam

  1. Start the server with node server.js and open the HTTPS URL. Install the PWA if you want home-screen + notifications.
  2. Allow camera, microphone (for sound alerts), and notifications when asked.
  3. Click Open camera tab — that tab is a clean full-screen camera, made to be shared — or use this camera.
  4. Live boxes appear on people (girl / boy, or a name you enrolled), animals (cat, dog, …), and other objects. The caption under the video describes what is happening.
  5. The DVR saves video locally as soon as detection is running. Sound, movement, falls, theft, or fights email a 10-second GIF plus the scene analysis.
  6. Connect Google under DVR to send those emails from your Gmail. Use Share for a LAN link and QR code.
  7. Open Videos to play every saved clip.

Facebook profile pages are not scraped. Save the profile photo and enroll it, or paste a direct image URL. While Facebook is on the shared screen, faces in photos and videos are still detected live.
